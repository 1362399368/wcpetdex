# Artwork and attribution

The project source code is distributed under the repository's MIT license. Petdex artwork and third-party character designs are not covered by that license and remain subject to their respective rights holders and terms.

The default pet is [噜噜](https://petdex.dev/pets/lulu-capybara-2), submitted to Petdex by gitcjp. Its sprite is fetched from the official Petdex asset host during a build and checked against a fixed SHA-256 digest. The source branch does not redistribute that sprite; a built package necessarily contains the generated pet animation strips. Check the rights holder's terms before further distribution or commercial use.

The separately fetched Petdex catalog snapshot is attributed in `Resources/PETDEX-SOURCES.txt`.
