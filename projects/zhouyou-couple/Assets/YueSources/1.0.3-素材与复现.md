# 玥 1.0.3 素材与复现

本版使用 Codex 内置 image_gen 生成关键姿态；未使用 CLI / API 密钥路径。保留的生成原图均在本目录，最终输出为 `Resources/DefaultPet/pet.json` 和 `spritesheet.png`。

## 当前重建入口

运行 `python3 Assets/YueSources/build_refined_pet.py`，依赖 Pillow。输出 v4 RGBA 图集 1920×6032、单格 192×208、29 行、286 个有效帧，15 fps。行 9、10 各 8 帧，其他行各 10 帧。语义映射保持兼容，不更改插件交互规则。

不要用历史 `build_default_pet.py` 重建新版，它会输出旧图集。

## 画面稳定方法

- 右行走用同一幅画的上半身和两条分层腿；膝盖和脚踝围绕固定关节转动，保留大腿、小腿长度。两腿相位错开 5 帧，交换支撑与摆动。
- 左行走是右行走逐帧水平镜像，杜绝左右朝向错配。
- 整个行走循环共用同一个头部位图和统一缩放比例；仅做最多 2 像素上下移动。
- 正面待机、玩手机、抱臂保留同一头部外轮廓和下半身，修改相应身体区域，消除逐帧脸变小、腿变长的拉伸。
- 坐姿按自然较低高度安置，不放大到站姿等高。眨眼约每 5.2 / 5.6 秒安排一次，不按每个动作周期连续眨眼。
- `Review/gait-joints.json` 保存最终关节、比例和位移；`Review/` 内保留逐帧总览及动态预览。透明边缘与有效帧经测试；缩放、旋转只用于装配生成的角色素材。

## 复现提示词（整理版）

以下为本轮生成约束的整理，供后续 AI 继续制图；生成仍需人工校准，不能直接把 AI 原图当作合格动画交付。

### 1. 行走关键姿态 — walk-cycle-generated.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: transparent 2D desktop-pet key-cel sheet. Use the supplied Yue character as identity reference: chibi young woman, long brown hair, round glasses, light blue short-sleeve shirt, black pleated skirt, pale backpack, white socks and pale sneakers. Create ten right-facing three-quarter walking/running phases arranged five columns by two rows. Show alternate contact, passing, lift and recovery of the near and far legs, not one split-leg pose shifted or stretched. Keep skull, glasses, face, shirt, skirt hem, thigh and shin lengths consistent. Full character visible, fixed camera and scale, truly transparent background, no text, no cell borders, no shadows or extra limbs.

最终图集从第一格提取同一人物的刚性层构建循环，而没有直接轮播尺寸可能不一致的十格。

### 2. 正面关键姿态 — standing-keys-generated.png / standing-keys-cutout.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Make a three-column by two-row sheet of the exact same Yue character at equal scale, full body. Row 1: neutral standing with eyes open; the identical pose with closed eyes for a slow blink; a small shoulder-height wave. Row 2: arms crossed with a mild pout; holding and looking at a phone with both hands, eyes open; identical phone pose, eyes closed. Lock the head outline, face placement, glasses, hairstyle, skirt length, short legs and shoes. Real transparent alpha, no background, no captions, no grid. Background-extraction follow-up: preserve the character art while removing only the backdrop and retaining clean semi-transparent hair edges.

### 3. 互动姿态 — emote-keys-generated.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Same Yue girl, three columns by two rows, transparent key-art sheet. Poses in order: naturally lower seated thoughtful pose; seated hugging knees with a mild pout; happy small jump with open arms; high wave with one bent leg; blowing a small heart; right-facing standing side profile. Keep head/skull size, round glasses, hair color, clothing, skirt length and limb proportions consistent across poses. Do not stretch a seated pose to standing height. No text, borders, scenery or duplicated limbs.

### 4. 转身姿态 — turn-keys-generated.png

Use case: identity-preserve. Same Yue girl, three full-body views arranged in one row: back view, rear three-quarter facing right, front three-quarter facing right. Consistent physical head size, height, hair, shirt, skirt, backpack and short legs. True transparent background, no labels, no cropping. These are turning keys, not different character designs.

## 本版保留的运行约束

确认对方在线时使用第 8 行、不自动贴边；待回应生气优先于在线待机。左右拖动、消息反馈、抚摸、情侣互动、边缘遮挡均沿用插件逻辑。图集相同尺寸，解码像素数与 1.0.2 相同；继续使用位打包命中缓冲、后台释放等既有内存优化。

安装后重启微信。若选中的是以前手工导入的玥，在外观设置已下载页选择内置玥或导入本次动画包。插件不会覆盖用户自选导入素材。
